About REL

The Relations, Entrepreneurship and Leadership club brings LU students together to meet people, try ideas and practice leading. Everything we run is open to every student.

Events on campus

Competitions, activities and one flagship event with a guest speaker, companies and a buffet.

Online workshops

Short live sessions on skills that matter in a real career.

Members and ambassadors

A community of official members, with ambassadors who represent REL across campus.

Partners and startups

Companies and organizations that open doors, with a startup accelerator as our long-term goal.
